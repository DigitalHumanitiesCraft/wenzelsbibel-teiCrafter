# Notice for fol1r.xml

`fol1r.xml` is a verbatim excerpt of fol. 1r from the TEI of Codex 2759 (Die Wenzelsbibel, PID `o:wen.codex-2759`) as published on the edition server of the Austrian National Library, edition.onb.ac.at. The text from the `pb` of fol. 1r to the `pb` of fol. 1v is copied byte for byte together with the enclosing `div` and `p` elements. Only the TEI header and the closing tags were added for the spike.
